Word-prediction / swipe dictionaries (en.dict, fa.dict, ckb.dict)
=================================================================
Converted by tools/build_dicts.py from the word lists of the kafe_keyboard /
CleverKeys project (https://github.com/tribixbite/cleverkeys). Every list was
modified: filtered, truncated and its frequencies re-quantised to 1..255.

en.dict  - CleverKeys English list: wordfreq (CC BY-SA 4.0) with AOSP LatinIME
           and spell-checker inclusion oracles.
fa.dict  - CleverKeys Persian list: Leipzig Corpora Collection counts
           (CC BY 4.0) blended with wordfreq (CC BY-SA 4.0); Lilak inclusion
           oracle (Apache-2.0); LDNOOBW-reviewed profanity blocklist.
ckb.dict - Central Kurdish (Sorani): kww Kurdish Wikipedia word list by
           Dolan Heris (MIT), counted from Kurdish Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0);
           KurdishHunspell ckb-Arab inclusion oracle by Sina Ahmadi
           (CC BY-SA 4.0).
